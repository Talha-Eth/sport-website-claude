How reminders work

  • 1. Find an upcoming match and press “Remind me”.
  • 2. Choose email or on-site delivery for each match.
  • 3. Your reminder time is applied to every saved match.
  • 4. The bell in the header always shows how many are active.

Email and browser notifications are simulated in this project so the site stays on free tools. The delivery choice and countdowns are fully functional.
