About the project

Reliable football information, all in one place.

Matchday is the Sports Information Platform proposed for our Introduction to Information & Communication Technologies course: a nine-page football website that brings live scores, fixtures, league tables and player statistics together, with reminders so fans never miss kick-off.

What Matchday does

Nine connected pages

Live scores

Matches are grouped as live, upcoming and finished, with the live minute refreshing on the page.

Player stats

Season totals and match-by-match ratings for every player, with profiles and side-by-side comparison.

Match reminders

Save any upcoming fixture, choose email or on-site delivery and follow a live countdown.

Search & filters

Filter by league, date, team and position; sort every table and search clubs, players and matches.

Data & credits

Sample data

Match & player data

Sample dataset built for this project: 5 leagues, 40 clubs, 560 players and 280 fixtures with simulated results.

Live data source (next phase)

Free football API such as football-data.org or API-Football, with caching to stay inside the free tier.

Built with

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript and localStorage. No frameworks, no paid services, no accounts.

Club badges & colours

Crests are original inline SVG monograms using club colours for identification only; no official artwork is included.

Type & icons

Barlow and Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts; interface icons drawn as inline SVG by the team.
